    Impact

    • Manual recomputations fully eliminated, with no further billing-data incidents.
    • No further IP blacklisting, with all emails delivered within SLA.

How I lead

Engineering focused on business outcomes

Three principles I apply to every team I lead.

01 · OWNERSHIP

Teams accountable for outcomes

I assign teams complete problems rather than isolated tasks, which enables them to go beyond the initial scope. The Klarna integration was not on the roadmap: the team identified it, we made the decision jointly and it launched ahead of Black Friday.

02 · GROWTH

Developing people and future leaders

Clear expectations and real responsibility from day one. Three people on my team have grown into lead roles. When our QA engineer joined, we set the move from manual to automated testing as a core objective; today they are QA Lead.

03 · DECISIONS

Technical decisions driven by business needs

I prioritise the problems with financial impact and resolve them within existing constraints. At Trovit, company policy prevented validation in the Java consumer; I moved validation into the deployment pipeline, eliminating manual billing corrections.
