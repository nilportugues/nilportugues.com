How I lead

Engineering focused on business outcomes

Four principles behind how I run engineering.

01 · PRIORITISATION

Revenue first, delivered in increments

I break every initiative into phases and start with whatever has the greatest impact on revenue, shipping small increments continuously, behind feature flags and measurable from day one. When the checkout was transferred to my team 18 months behind schedule, we prioritised checkout and ecommerce tracking, the two pieces Paper needed to sell and to measure, and met the December date.

02 · OWNERSHIP

Teams accountable for outcomes

I assign teams complete problems rather than isolated tasks, which enables them to go beyond the initial scope. Adding Klarna, for example, was pushed forward by the team: once they showed it was feasible, it was added to the roadmap.

03 · GROWTH

Developing people and future leaders

Clear expectations and real responsibility from day one. Three people on my team have grown into lead roles. When our QA engineer joined, we set the move from manual to automated testing as a core objective; today they are QA Lead.

04 · DECISIONS

Technical decisions driven by business needs

I prioritise the problems with financial impact and resolve them within existing constraints. At Trovit, company policy prevented validation in the Java consumer; I moved validation into the deployment pipeline, ending the recurring manual billing corrections.
